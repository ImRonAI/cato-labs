## Section #manifesto: A grand tourer, made rather than assembled.
### A grand tourer, made rather than assembled.

Aether Motors builds coachbuilt electric grand tourers. There is one model, the GT-01, and every one is commissioned. The body is formed from titanium and exposed carbon fibre; the drive is four independent motors; the battery is solid-state.

We hold ourselves to aerospace tolerances because a car that will outlive its first owner should be made that way. Nothing on the GT-01 is decorative. The lines exist because the air asked for them.

Read the engineering

## Section #engineering: Four motors. One intention.
Powertrain

### Four motors. One intention.

Torque is calculated for each wheel a thousand times a second and delivered through a 900-volt silicon-carbide inverter. The result is a car that corners as if the road were drawn for it.

[image: Aether GT-01 Carbon-Titanium Monocoque Chassis Cutaway showing quad axial-flux motors and T-bone solid-state battery]

Carbon-Titanium Monocoque Architecture · Quad Axial-Flux Drive · T-Bone Solid-State Dielectric Pack

-
i

#### Independent torque vectoring

Each motor answers to its own wheel. Torque is recalculated at 1,000 Hz, so the GT-01 rotates into a corner before the driver has finished asking.

2.4 msResponse

-
ii

#### 900 V silicon-carbide inverter

The high-voltage architecture loses less to heat, which means peak output can be held lap after lap rather than glimpsed once.

99.2%Efficiency

-
iii

#### Solid-state thermal management

A proprietary dielectric fluid circulates through the pack, holding every cell at its preferred temperature from the Arctic to the Gulf.

−40° to +85°Operating range
