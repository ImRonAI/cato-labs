## Section #science: Reprogramming is a dial, not a switch. We turn it briefly, then let go.
Approach

### Reprogramming is a dial, not a switch. We turn it briefly, then let go.

Four factors — OCT4, SOX2, KLF4 and MYC — can return an adult cell to pluripotency. Held too long, they erase identity. Our work is in the timing: a transient pulse that rewinds epigenetic age while the cell remains what it is.

-
01

#### Modified OSKM mRNA

mRNA, not DNA. Nothing integrates into the genome. The message is translated for a limited window and then degrades on its own schedule — a half-life measured in hours.

-
02

#### Tissue-targeted lipid nanoparticles

Each programme uses a formulation built for its destination: the Alabaster Shell (LNP-AS4) for liver, a cardio-targeted peptide conjugate (LNP-CT2) for heart, and a blood-brain-barrier shuttle (LNP-BBB) for microglia.

-
03

#### Transient by design

Dose, cadence and the modified transcript itself are tuned so cells pass through partial reprogramming without reaching pluripotency. Avoiding teratoma risk is the design constraint, not an afterthought.

-
04

#### Measured one cell at a time

We read the outcome with single-cell methylation mapping and single-cell RNA sequencing: epigenetic clocks, senescence markers such as p16INK4a and p21, and the longevity-associated transcripts SIRT1 and FOXO3.

Every programme on this site is investigational. No Solstice Bio therapy is approved by any regulator, and nothing here should be read as a promise of benefit. The data we can share lives in the data room, under confidentiality.

Programme · Phase II

### SB-104 Hepatocyte rejuvenation

Targeted lipid-nanoparticle delivery of a modified OSKM mRNA cocktail to hepatocytes, designed to reverse hepatic fibrosis and metabolic decline.

TissueLiver

StagePhase II

FormulationAlabaster Shell (LNP-AS4)

Transcript half-life36.4 hours (modified mRNA)

#### Indication

Hepatic fibrosis and metabolic decline in end-stage liver disease.

#### How it works

SB-104 is the most advanced programme in the pipeline. It uses the Alabaster Shell formulation, LNP-AS4, which was developed in Boston for hepatic tropism: the particle is taken up preferentially by hepatocytes after intravenous infusion.

The transcript is a modified OSKM cocktail with a half-life measured in hours. Dose and cadence are set so that cells pass through a partial reprogramming window and return to a hepatocyte identity with a younger methylation profile.

In the Phase II cohort, outcomes are read with single-cell RNA sequencing of pre- and post-infusion biopsies, alongside epigenetic clock measurements, senescence markers (p16INK4a, p21) and the longevity-associated transcripts SIRT1 and FOXO3. The study is monitored under GCP by Geneva clinical oversight.

Development stage

- Discovery
- Preclinical
- Phase I
- Phase II
- Phase III

Phase II means SB-104 is being studied for safety and for early signs of activity in people. It is not an approved treatment and no clinical benefit has been established. Cohort readouts are shared with qualified investigators under confidentiality.

Programme · Phase I/II

### SB-209 Myocardial epigenetic reset

Cardiomyocyte-specific transient reprogramming intended to restore ventricular compliance after infarction.

TissueHeart

StagePhase I/II

FormulationCardio-targeted peptide conjugate (LNP-CT2)

Transcript half-life28.2 hours (modified mRNA)

#### Indication

Ventricular remodelling and ischaemic scarring after myocardial infarction.

#### How it works

SB-209 pairs the OSKM transcript with LNP-CT2, a lipid nanoparticle carrying a cardiac-homing peptide. The aim is to confine reprogramming to cardiomyocytes in and around the infarct border zone.

Because the heart tolerates very little error, SB-209 uses the shortest-lived transcript in the pipeline. The programme is designed around a brief exposure, measured in hours, with cardiac function and rhythm monitored throughout the study.

The Phase I/II design combines a dose-escalation safety phase with an early look at functional measures. Readouts are reviewed by the Geneva clinical team under GCP.

Development stage

- Discovery
- Preclinical
- Phase I
- Phase II
- Phase III

Phase I/II means the first participants have received SB-209 under a safety-first protocol. Reaching a study endpoint is not the same as demonstrating benefit, and SB-209 is not approved anywhere.

SB-401 is the pipeline’s most accessible tissue and, for that reason, an important one: skin biopsies allow direct, repeated measurement of what transient reprogramming does to a human cell population over time.

### SB-312 Microglial and synaptic programme

A blood-brain-barrier-penetrant formulation targeting microglia, with the long-term aim of restoring synaptic density in neurodegenerative disease.

TissueBrain

StagePreclinical · GLP toxicology

FormulationBBB-shuttling Alabaster Shell (LNP-BBB)

Transcript half-life42.0 hours (modified mRNA)

#### Indication

Microglial rejuvenation and synaptic density in neurodegenerative disease.

#### How it works

SB-312 is the most ambitious programme and the earliest. Its formulation, LNP-BBB, is a variant of the Alabaster Shell engineered to cross the blood-brain barrier and reach microglia, the brain’s resident immune cells.

The scientific hypothesis is that rejuvenated microglia support synaptic maintenance. Testing that hypothesis in people requires first establishing safety, which is where the programme sits today.

SB-312 is in GLP toxicology, the formal, regulated animal safety studies that must be completed before a first-in-human application can be filed.

Development stage

- Preclinical
- Phase I
- Phase II
- Phase III

Preclinical means SB-312 has not been given to any person. GLP toxicology is a prerequisite for applying to begin a Phase I study, not evidence of effect.
