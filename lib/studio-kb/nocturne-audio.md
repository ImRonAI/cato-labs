## Section #principle: A reference instrument has no sound of its own. It is a room with nothing in it but the recording.
### A reference instrument has no sound of its own. It is a room with nothing in it but the recording.

Most hi-fi adds. Warmth, air, scale, a signature the brochure can name. We spend our time taking things out: distortion, noise, crosstalk, the half-decibel ripple that tells you a driver is there.

What remains is the performance as it was captured. Nothing more, which is the hardest thing to build.

How we measure it

-
<0.0003%

Distortion

THD+N, the whole of what the amplifier adds that the recording did not contain.

-
132dB

Noise floor

Signal-to-noise, A-weighted. The silence between notes is as quiet as the room.

-
−118dB

Crosstalk

Between balanced channels at 1 kHz. Left stays left.

## Section #engineering: Built by hand. Judged by instruments.
Engineering

### Built by hand. Judged by instruments.

Every unit spends seventy-two hours burning in, then a day on the bench. It is measured as an individual, not as a sample of a batch, and the sheet travels with it.

The bench is an Audio Precision APx555. The numbers below are the atelier's published figures; they are what we will show you on your own unit when you collect it.

Read the bench procedure

- 01

#### Driver matching

Planar diaphragms are measured in pairs before assembly. A left and right that do not match within tolerance never meet.

- 02

#### Hand assembly, Geneva

Enclosures are CNC-milled from aluminium and finished by hand. Each Mono-A is wired point to point; the 6SN7GT input pair is selected and matched on the bench.

- 03

#### Seventy-two hours

Burn-in under signal, so that what we measure is what you will hear after the first week, not before it.

- 04

#### Individual verification

Frequency response, THD+N, noise, crosstalk and output impedance are swept on the APx555. The sheet is signed and packed with the instrument.

Frequency response

20 Hz to 20 kHz, deviation within ±0.05 dB. The shaded band is the tolerance. The line is a representative unit.

## Reference N-1 Planar

Planar-magnetic headphone

A third-generation planar-magnetic headphone with a diaphragm thin enough to be measured in nanometres, driven from both sides so that it moves as one surface.

$6,800Built to order in Geneva. Ships with its individual measurement sheet.

Hear it in a listening room
Ask about this instrument

### Specification

- **Driver**: Planar-magnetic, 3rd generation

- **Diaphragm**: Nanometre-scale membrane

- **Magnets**: Dual-sided neodymium arrays

- **Impedance**: 32 Ω nominal

- **Frequency response**: 5 Hz – 52 kHz

- **Termination**: Balanced 4.4 mm

- **Cable**: Hand-wound OFC

- **Enclosure**: CNC-milled aluminium

- **Source support**: DSD512 native

### Construction

The diaphragm is the entire argument. Make it light enough and the music stops having to push mass around; place the magnets on both faces and the force is even across the surface rather than strongest at the edges. The N-1 is built around that pair of decisions and very little else.

The enclosures are milled from aluminium and finished by hand in Geneva. Pairs are matched before assembly, so that the two drivers you receive have already been measured against each other.

## Mono-A Reference

Class-A triode monoblock

A pure Class-A triode monoblock with no global feedback. One chassis per channel, a matched 6SN7GT input stage, and output transformers wound for it alone.

$14,500Built to order in Geneva. Ships with its individual measurement sheet.

Hear it in a listening room
Ask about this instrument

### Specification

- **Topology**: Pure Class-A, zero feedback

- **Input stage**: Matched 6SN7GT, hand-selected

- **Output transformers**: Custom, grain-oriented silicon steel

- **Construction**: Dual-mono, one chassis per channel

- **Conversion**: 32-bit / 768 kHz

- **Output impedance**: 0.04 Ω

- **Damping factor**: > 800 into 32 Ω

- **THD+N**: < 0.0003 %

- **Signal-to-noise**: 132 dB, A-weighted

- **Crosstalk**: −118 dB at 1 kHz

### Construction

Feedback is the easy way to a good number: measure the error and subtract it. We chose not to, because the correction arrives a moment late and the ear hears the moment. The Mono-A gets its figures the slow way, from a triode input stage running in Class-A and transformers designed around it.

Each channel lives in its own chassis with its own supply, so the left never borrows current from the right. The input pair is selected and matched on the bench, then the whole instrument is measured again as a finished thing.

## Read the bench procedure
### The bench procedure

Every instrument that leaves Geneva has been measured as an individual. The following is what happens between final assembly and the box.

#### Burn-in

Seventy-two hours under a varied signal at listening level. Planar diaphragms and triode stages both settle; we measure after they have.

#### Analyser

Audio Precision APx555. Balanced in and out, matched to the instrument's termination.

#### Sweeps

- Frequency response20 Hz – 20 kHz, ±0.05 dB
- THD+N< 0.0003 %
- Signal-to-noise132 dB, A-weighted
- Crosstalk−118 dB at 1 kHz
- Output impedance0.04 Ω, damping > 800 into 32 Ω

#### The sheet

The plots are printed, signed by the engineer who ran them, and packed with the instrument. When you collect it in a listening room, we will put the sheet beside the unit and show you the same sweep live.
