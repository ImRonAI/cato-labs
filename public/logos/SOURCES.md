## Not from Simple Icons, and why

Simple Icons `16.36.0` has no `salesforce`, `openai`, `xai`, `unsloth`, `axolotl`, `guru` or `sharepoint` icons (`openaigym` exists but is a different product). Those slugs were sourced from the brand owners directly as listed above.
